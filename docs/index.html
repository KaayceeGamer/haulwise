Haulwise
CurseForge Source code

Every kill makes everyone's drop rates better.

Haulwise is a free addon for WoW Forever that shows what everything you carry is worth, which gear is an upgrade, where the gold is, and the way to your next trainer. Its drop rates and recipe sources come from players like you. In English, German and French.

Share your data

Addons can't reach the internet, so Haulwise never sends anything by itself. Sharing takes about ten seconds:

  1. In game, press Share data in the Haulwise window, or type /haul export.
  2. Press Ctrl+C to copy.
  3. Paste it below. With the box ticked it's shared straight away, and this page remembers the tick for next time.

Thank you, it's in.

Share again whenever you like. Your new upload replaces your old one, so nothing is counted twice.

Get the newest community data

Haulwise brings the pool along and gets a fresh copy every day through CurseForge. To get the latest numbers right now, copy them here and type /haul import in game.

What the addon does

Bags
Values every item at the best of vendor, auction house and disenchant, and says the best move for each: sell, auction, disenchant, or send it to an alt. When your bags are full, it offers a one-click swap and never touches crafting materials, quest items or upgrades. Leveling Enchanting? Disenchant for mats puts the dust first.
Gear
Checks new gear against what you and your alts wear, using your Pawn scales or built-in stat weights. It finds upgrades for sale on the auction house, ranked by upgrade per gold, and marks the best quest reward to pick.
Farming
Ranks mobs and zones by gold per hour for your character, from real drop rates and your own kill speed.
Crafting
Profit per recipe, a shopping list for what you want to make, the cheapest way to skill up, and recipes worth learning with where to get them.
Waypoints
An arrow to the nearest trainer who teaches your next rank, or to the vendor who sells a recipe. Uses TomTom when you have it.
Auction house
Deals under the usual price, vendor flips, price trends, and a suggested price when you sell. You always press the buttons yourself.

Getting started

Install Haulwise from CurseForge, then spend five minutes so it knows what you have:

  1. Open each of your profession windows once.
  2. Visit a trade goods vendor once.
  3. Press Scan AH at the auction house. Once a day is ideal.
  4. Log in on each of your characters once.
  5. Open your bank and mailbox once.

Then just play. Open Haulwise with the chest button on your minimap or /haul; the i button inside explains everything.

What gets shared

Only what you paste
The addon can't reach the internet. Data leaves your PC only when you paste it here.
What's in an export
Kills, loot, coins, levels and zones per mob; the recipes your characters know and what they're made from; recipes you've seen at trainers and vendors with their prices; what vendors charge for reagents; where the trainers and vendors you opened stand (map and coordinates); and a random id the addon made up, so a new upload replaces your old one.
What's kept
Those numbers, stored under a one-way hash of the random id. Character names are thrown away. No accounts, no email, no cookies, no tracking.
Your IP address
Never stored. A hash of it that changes every day limits spam uploads for one day, then it's deleted.
