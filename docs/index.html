Share your kills

  1. In game, type /haul export (or press Export in the Haulwise window).
  2. Press Ctrl+C to copy, then paste it here.

You can upload again any time. Your new upload replaces your old one, so nothing is counted twice.

Get the latest community data

Every Haulwise release on CurseForge already includes the community data, rebuilt each week. Want it sooner? Copy it here and paste it in game with /haul import.

Privacy

Your export contains kills, loot, coins, levels and zones per mob, the recipes you've seen at trainers and vendors (with their prices), what vendors charge for reagents, and a random id the addon made up. That id is how a new upload replaces your old one.

What's stored: those numbers, under a one-way hash of the random id. Your character name in the export is thrown away. No account names, no email, no cookies, no tracking.

Your IP address is never stored. A hash of it, which changes every day, is used for one day to limit spam uploads, then deleted.

Nothing is sent automatically. The addon can't reach the internet; data only leaves your PC if you paste it here yourself. Uploads can't be linked to you, your character or your account. Haulwise is a free hobby project with no ads and no money involved.
